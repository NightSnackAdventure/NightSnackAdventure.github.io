Class Features
Evocation Savant
2 free evocation spells (lvl 1–2) in spellbook at lvl 3. 1 free evocation spell per new spell level unlocked going forward.
Potent Cantrip
When a cantrip misses or target succeeds on its save, they still take half damage.
Ritual Adept
Can cast any spell with the Ritual tag from spellbook without preparing it.
Arcane Recovery
Once per day after a Short Rest, recover spell slots with combined level no more than half your Wizard level (round up).
Scholar
While studying magic, you also specialized in another field of study. Choose one of the following skills in which you have proficiency: Arcana, History, Investigation, Medicine, Nature, or Religion. You have Expertise in the chosen skill.
Memorize Spell
Once per short rest, swap one prepared spell for another from spellbook.
Resourceful (Human)
Gain Heroic Inspiration after every long rest — a reroll usable on any die.

Advantage on CON saves to maintain concentration. Cast spells as opportunity attacks. Perform somatic components while holding weapons/shield. Therius does not let go of a spell just because something hit him.

Magic Items
Staff of Charming
Staff, Rare · Arcane Focus · Requires Attunement · 10 charges (regains 1d8+2/dawn) · Legacy
Cast Charm Person, Command, or Comprehend Languages (1 charge each, DC 15). Resist Enchantment: turn a failed enchantment save into a success, 1×/dawn. Reflect Enchantment: on a successful save vs. enchantment, spend 1 charge to turn the spell back on the caster.

It's a family heirloom from his father's side. His mother obtained it for him after the Incident. He was mortified, yet grateful.
Ouroboros Amulet
Wondrous Item, Uncommon · Jade serpent devouring its own tail
Advantage on saving throws against being charmed. Cast Suggestion 1×/dawn (DC 13).

Carved in the likeness of a serpent swallowing its own tail, worn by serpentfolk mystics and worshippers of dark gods. A token of good faith. He doesn't know who the rightful owner is, and what befell them. He keeps it for the advantage on charm saves and absolutely no other reason.
Nightcap of Pleasant Dreams
Trinket, Common, rolled at character creation
Grants pleasant dreams when worn to sleep.

He looks adorable in it. It keeps his dreams peaceful. He will not sleep without it.
